# Stick It Together

A party game built at SotonHack 2026 where two teams race to connect two unrelated words through a chain of associations.

## Game Flow

- 2 words appear on screen
- Buzzer goes off at random time
- First person to pick it up lists their connection chain from the first word to the second
- Chain gets validated and scored on points (1-10) 
- The player with the least amount of points at the end wins
